Scope: https://www.alphalab.site and its public WordPress REST API (`hacktechentertainment-iylwc.wpcomstaging.com/wp-json/wp/v2`). 33 requests total, 2 s apart, browser UA. robots.txt (re-read today) disallows `/api/`, `/admin`, `/wp-admin`, `/wp-login.php`. ## Executive summary

1. **The "strategy" feature is a live crypto trading-strategy dashboard, not an article series.** It lives at `/category/crypto/trading` (nav label 加密貨幣 — 交易研究, page title 「交易策略 — AlphaLab」). It shows 4 bots (BTC/ETH × Martingale spot / trend perpetual) running live with 5,000 USDT each on OKX, plus 28 published TradingView backtests compared with Buy & Hold and monthly/weekly DCA.
2. **No WordPress posts.** The WP category `trading` has count 0. A custom Next.js client page takes over the category URL and polls a same-origin `/api/trading-dashboard` about every 60 s. Research release `20261018-multiasset-research`, reviewed 2026-10-28.
3. **How it makes money:** an "OKX 跟單這個策略" (copy this strategy) flow. Step 1 is to sign up for OKX through the affiliate link (marked `rel="sponsored"`); then the user searches for the trader name in the OKX app. The page also pushes a LINE group with free and VIP courses, which leads to /courses and /consulting. The sidebar did not change.
4. **The strategy is closed-source and comes from a third party ("Justin").** The signals mix MACD/KD/RSI/DMI on a 6 h Binance chart. They publish an audit page with SHA-256 hashes and a verification script, and say plainly that repainting or look-ahead bias has not been ruled out.
5. **Nav and the affiliate sidebar are identical to 10-29.** The 交易研究 nav entry was already there on 10-29; we just didn't open it.
6. **Pipeline outage on 10-29.** Nothing was published from 10-29 00:35 to 10-30 03:45 UTC (~27 h): no 10-29 market digest, crypto digest or AI batch. A catch-up burst followed: 17 posts between 03:45 and 05:26 UTC (11 earnings checks in 11 min). The crypto slug collided again (`…-2`).
7. **Logos are self-hosted, not from a logo API.** Same-origin static files `/logos/earnings/{TICKER}.{svg|png|jpg|webp}` (663 files referenced, about 80% SVG). They are plain `<img>` tags (not next/image) served from Vercel's CDN. No clearbit, brandfetch or favicon hosts anywhere.
8. **Sitemap churn and a timezone bug.** 31 URLs dropped and 46 older URLs re-added since 10-29. Post `lastmod` values are now Taipei local time labelled `Z` (+8 h wrong).
9. **Top picks for PreBell:** a self-hosted logo set with text-chip fallback; a public "receipts" page for verified numbers; and catch-up runs keyed by market date. **Avoid:** live trading or copy-trade features, and client-only SEO pages.

## 1. The strategy feature — what it is

**URL and page shell** (evidence: `raw/probe-category_crypto_trading.html`)
- `https://www.alphalab.site/category/crypto/trading`. Title 「交易策略 — AlphaLab」, meta description: 「BTC、ETH 四策略的即時持倉、成交紀錄與歷史回測，並列比較 Buy & Hold 與 DCA。」
- `robots: index, follow`, canonical set, listed in sitemap.xml (priority 0.6).
- Server HTML contains only `<template data-dgst="BAILOUT_TO_CLIENT_SIDE_RENDERING">` and 「載入交易策略…」. Everything renders on the client, so crawlers see an empty page.
- 4 page-specific chunks; the logic is in `/_next/static/chunks/ac4753e1afbef665.js` (134 KB) and the research manifest in `946bdebbf1a9ee79.js`.
- The WP category exists (`id 783123014`, slug `trading`, name 交易研究, **count 0**). Its ID sits just below tag IDs first used on 10-24, so the category was probably created around 10-22 to 10-24 (inference). Next.js overrides the category route with a custom page.

**The four strategies** (account map, verbatim keys from the bundle)

| Acct | Label | Instrument | Type | Live capital |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | BTC 馬丁複利 | BTC-USDT | spot, long only ("三方馬丁") | 5,000 USDT |
| B | BTC 力道複利 | BTC-USDT-SWAP | perpetual, long/short ("三方力道") | 5,000 USDT |
| C | ETH 馬丁複利 | ETH-USDT | spot, long only | 5,000 USDT |
| D | ETH 力道複利 | ETH-USDT-SWAP | perpetual, long/short | 5,000 USDT |

(Account D's `shortLabel` reads "ETH 馬丁", a copy bug in their config.)

**Rules shown in the in-page "策略說明" dialog** (copy is static in the bundle):
- **Signals:** Binance {asset}/USDT, 6 h chart. Direction comes from a composite of MACD, KD, RSI and DMI. Weights and thresholds are not public: 「各指標權重、週期、同向門檻與反轉公式未公開」.
- **Martingale (spot):** the core position is about 99% of equity on a long signal. When the trend weakens, it exits and opens a ~1% Martingale order. Each add is ~1.5× larger, with up to 8 adds and a 2% take-profit parameter. Branch settings: RSI 7 at 70/30, KD 7/3/3 with threshold 20, 30-minute reference period. Fees 0.10% per side.
- **Trend (perpetual):** one direction at a time at about 1× equity (exchange margin set to 3x). On reversal it closes the position, then reopens the other way. Fees 0.05% per side.
- **Author attribution:** 「AlphaLab 策略開發：Terry Chen & Justin Fu」. The design is reconstructed from "Justin"'s explanations, screenshots and trade lists: 「原始策略仍是閉源…也尚未證明沒有重繪或使用未來資料」.

**Data model, from client code** (endpoints only; I did not call them):
- `/api/trading-dashboard/accounts`: configured accounts plus execution state `{synchronized, blocked, reason, last_execution_at}`.
- `/accounts/{A-D}/performance?currency=USDT&from&to`.
- Live comparison at `resolution=minute|hour|daily`.
- Paginated records `side|status|limit|cursor` with tabs: positions, fills (實際成交), signals (策略訊號), closed trades.
- `/research/runs?account&kind=full_history|cold_start&status=verified|verified_with_source_gaps|partial|quarantined` and `/research/runs/{id}/comparison?resolution=6h|daily`.
- "Historical backtest + forward simulation" mode. The forward simulation runs from received signals; the dashboard has states such as `signal_catchup_pending` and `market_gap`.
- Polling: `setInterval(…, 6e4)`, skipped when `document.visibilityState === "hidden"`.
- Defensive parsing everywhere: 16 MB cap, shape validation, "Repeated cursor" guard, and a refusal to plot misaligned series (「時間未對齊的曲線不補值」).

**Backtests and the audit trail** (manifest in `946bdebbf1a9ee79.js`; public page `/trading-evidence/94dffcad…/publication-audit.html`, 4.9 KB, fetched)
- Manifest: `schemaVersion 1`, `reviewedAt 2026-10-28`, `researchRelease 20261018-multiasset-research`.
- Verification counts: 57 trade CSV files, 28 published runs, 358,352 independently recomputed NAV points, residual tolerance 1e-6, `independentSamples: false`.
- Windows: full history 2017-08-17 → 2026-10-17, 2018 bear, 2020 crash/recovery, 2021–22 bear, 2023 recovery, recent year, and calendar years 2021 and 2024 (ETH only).
- **Losing windows are published too.** A 2018: −54.69%. A, recent year: +3.84%. C, recent year: −10.82%. Full-history max drawdowns: 61–78%.
- Benchmarks use the same pre-funded capital. DCA deploys it over 12 months or 52 weeks of year one; slippage, funding, copy-trade profit share and liquidation are not simulated.
- The 23 MB evidence zip with `verify_release.py` is linked; I did not download it.

**Is it auto-generated?** The trading side is automated: live executor state, a signal feed and forward simulation. The explanatory copy is hand-written or agent-written static text compiled into the bundle, not generated per day. It is not an LLM content stream, and there is no `market-overview`-style template marker. The research appears to be a one-off release (10-18) that gets re-reviewed (10-28).

**Data it relies on:** exchange prices (Binance for signals, OKX for execution), TradingView Pine strategy exports, OKX account and fill data, and a TradingView advanced-chart iframe (`tradingview-widget.com`, locale zh_TW, BTC/ETH only). No equities, options or macro calendar on this page. The LINE "community" tab does show examples of macro event prep (PPI, payrolls, economic calendar).

## 5. Company-logo strip — how it's built
- **Source:** self-hosted static files in the Next.js repo (`public/logos/earnings/`), served same-origin as `https://www.alphalab.site/logos/earnings/{TICKER}.{ext}`.
  - Unique files referenced on `/earnings`: **526 .svg, 116 .png, 20 .jpg, 1 .webp**.
  - Ticker normalization: `BRK.B → BRK-B` (from the 10-29 notes).
  - The `logo_url` field in the earnings JSON always points at these files; no external URL appears.
- **Headers** (HEAD `/logos/earnings/AZN.svg`): `server: Vercel`, `x-vercel-cache: HIT`, `content-type: image/svg+xml`, `cache-control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`, 14,303 bytes. CDN-cached but revalidated on every request (they never set long caching).
- **Markup:**
  - Header strip: `<img src="/logos/earnings/AZN.svg" alt="AstraZeneca logo" class="h-4 w-auto max-w-[80px] object-contain">` inside a chip titled 「AstraZeneca（AZN）」, under a 盤前 / 盤後 / 時段待確認 label with an inline SVG icon.
  - `/earnings` grid: `loading="lazy"`, `max-h-8`, white rounded tile, and an sr-only text 「… 財報，前往官方網站」 that links to the company site (`rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`).
  - Plain `<img>`, **not** `next/image`; no width/height attributes, so layout can shift.
- **No logo API:** no clearbit, brandfetch, logo.dev or google s2/favicons host appears in the HTML or bundles. Mixed formats (PNG/JPG next to SVG) suggest the files were collected by hand or by an agent from company sites. Provenance is not stated (inference).
- Today's strip (10-30, 盤前): AZN, XOM, CVX, ABBV, BP, EXC.
