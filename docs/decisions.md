- **IPO watch, builder's rulings** (under "go ahead and build it", 2026-11-11; each reversible in the file named):
  - **Storage is D1 only**: five new tables (`migrations/0009_ipo_watch.sql`), no R2 bundle. The facts are a few rows per filing, and each job reads the whole state in one batch and writes in one, so its D1 use stays at 2-4 queries whatever the number of IPOs.
  - **Cover facts by code patterns, no model** (`crates/core/src/ipo/cover.rs`). Kept: exchange and symbol, shares offered, the company / selling-stockholder split (only when it adds up to the total), the underwriters' option to buy more shares, the price range (registration statements) or final price and pricing date (final prospectus), the cover date, and a first trading day only when the filing states it. Each fact keeps the cover's own words and is dropped unless those words are in the document and the number is written in them. The number is matched as the cover writes it ("825,000,000", "$70.00"), not by the number checker, because its ~30 Unicode patterns would take a cold read past the 10 ms CPU limit. For the same reason the cover patterns are ASCII mode.
  - **No deal-size dollar figure.** The fee exhibit's "maximum aggregate offering price" includes the underwriters' option and is a number for the SEC fee, not the deal size. It is not read; the page shows shares and the price range or final price.
  - **Which covers are read**: per IPO, only the newest registration statement (S-1, S-1/A, F-1, F-1/A) or final prospectus (424B4, 424B1), and only its first 160 KB (Anthropic's 2026-11-10 S-1/A is 14.8 MB; its cover ends near byte 85,000). Older amendments and DRS filings are timeline only. At most 3 covers per hourly run. Each attempt is written to D1 before the read, so a read killed for CPU still counts; after 3 failed attempts the filing is `failed` and one `ipo-cover:<slug>` error alert is raised. A cover that yields no facts raises one `ipo-cover:<slug>` warning, and the page keeps the earlier facts.
  - **Status** (`crates/core/src/ipo/resolve.rs`): `withdrawn` when an `RW` is the newest offering form; `listed` once a Yahoo close is stored; `priced` once a 424B4/424B1 is on file; `range_set` when the current cover states a range; `filed` for any registration statement or DRS. With no such form there is no row.
  - **The date rule** (`resolve.rs`, the bar being "confirmed never rests on a guess"), first that applies:
    1. *confirmed, trading*: the symbol's first regular session close from Yahoo (the recap's read);
    2. *confirmed, filing*: a filing that states the first trading day in its own words ("is expected to begin trading ... on November 5, 2026"; "on or about" never counts);
    3. *expected, filing*: the next trading day (by `clock`) after the pricing date a final prospectus states ("Prospectus dated ..."); else, with an `8-A12B` on file, the next trading day after the SEC's `EFFECT` notice. A rule, so never "confirmed";
    4. *reported, news*: the date the newest `expected_date` news item gives, until the day after it; a report whose day has passed is dropped (the row reads "date not set"), never replaced by an older report;
    5. otherwise none. A withdrawn IPO has no date.
  - **The news sweep** (`crates/core/src/ipo/news.rs`): queued by the earnings poll (not the premarket job, which has no follow-up queue) from 07:00 ET and from 16:30 ET on trading days, one IPO per `ipo:sweep` message on the jobs queue (not the serial earnings queue, so a 30-60 s model call never delays a poll). Each sweep is one Tavily search ("<name> IPO", news from the last 14 days, 5 articles, with Tavily's raw article text as "the fetched article": no separate page fetch), one model call, and one for Chinese. Articles without a publication date are dropped before the model sees them. Sweeps stop for a withdrawn IPO and 30 days after the first session.
  - **The item checks** (code, drop on failure): the quote is word for word in the article (`anchor::verify_quote`); the article names the company; the outlet is the article's site (a table of about 30 outlets with their other names, else the site's own name: "TechCrunch" for techcrunch.com); every number in the statement is in the quote (`numbers::check`); the statement has none of "could", "might", "may", "likely", "probably", "should", "recommend", "beat", "missed", "consensus", "estimate", "forecast", "undervalued", "overvalued", "bargain", "pop", "soar", "buy"; a valuation's figure is among the quote's numbers and the quote speaks of value; an expected date is written in the quote (month and day, and the year if written) next to "trading", "list" or "debut", on or after the article's day; a view is the speaker's words inside quotation marks in the article, the speaker named within 600 characters of them, and stored with no statement (a paraphrase is dropped). The model gives no figure that is stored without its quote.
  - **Caps**: per IPO, valuation 10, expected_date 5, company_move 8, view 6, newest by publication day; the page shows at most 3 views. Dedupe by (IPO, kind, URL), first stored wins. At most 8 proposed items per sweep.
  - **Tavily allowance**: the site's own Tavily use is up to about 42 searches a trading day (premarket 12, update 12, recap 13, calendar 5), about 880 in a 21-day month before retries; Tavily's free plan is 1,000 credits a month. The sweeps are capped at 100 searches a calendar month (kv `ipo_searches`, `MONTHLY_SEARCH_CAP`), about two IPOs' worth; at the cap, sweeps stop until the next month with one `ipo-search-cap` warning a day.
  - **Chinese**: one model call per sweep for the kept statements, each number-checked against its quote; a failing one stays NULL and the page falls back to English. Quotes stay in their original language.
  - **After listing** (`crates/core/src/ipo/trading.rs`): the recap's batched Yahoo close read adds the symbols of priced or listed IPOs only while its last 50-symbol batch has room (32 slots with today's 518-symbol universe), so no extra request. The IPO is never a mover. The page's change against the IPO price is computed by code from the stored close and the 424B4's price, both stored beside it. **No opening price in v1**: the recap's quote read has none, so `ipo_trading.open` stays NULL; adding it means a field on the shared `Quote` type (about 40 call sites).
